Frequently asked

How do the controls work?

Tap anywhere to jump; tap again in the air to double jump, and hold to hover. Swipe up or down where the track forks to pick a route. With RUN set to AUTO you only jump; with MANUAL you also steer. PAD shows an on-screen game pad. In the Arena, the left side of the screen moves and the right side aims and fires.

I bought the Full Game but levels are still locked.

Finish level 10 or open a locked mode, then tap RESTORE PURCHASE while signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with. The Full Game is a one-time purchase and restores for free on every device with that Apple ID. If it still won't unlock, email me with the order ID from your Apple receipt.

I asked a parent to buy it.

With Ask to Buy, the game says it's waiting. It unlocks by itself as soon as the request is approved.

I lost my progress.

Progress is saved on your device only, with no cloud sync, so uninstalling the app clears it. Setting up a new device from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one brings it across. Your Full Game purchase is never lost: use RESTORE PURCHASE.

I ran out of lives.

A life comes back every three minutes, up to twenty. The Arcade and Arena earn extra lives, and the Light Shop sells them for light you collect in the campaign.

The app crashed or a level seems impossible.

Please email ryanevans23@gmail.com with the level number and what happened.
